# Code of conduct

Mochi welcomes participants of every background and level of experience.

Treat people with respect. Discuss the technical issue rather than the person,
accept constructive feedback, give credit for others' work, and help newcomers
participate. Respect other people's privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct,
and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. These
expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces
where people participate on behalf of the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause a discussion, or restrict
participation. Repeated or serious violations can lead to removal from project
spaces. Decisions should be proportionate and considerate of affected people.

For a concern, contact a repository maintainer privately through a contact method
they make available on their GitHub profile. GitHub's reporting tools can also be
used for abusive content or accounts. Do not repeat private details in a public
report. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and avoid disclosing more
information than is necessary to respond.
